## 🟢 General FAQ (All Apps)

**Q: How do I request a refund?**  
A: Payments and subscriptions are managed entirely by Apple. We cannot process refunds directly. Please request a refund via [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Q: The app crashes or won't open.**  
A: Try reinstalling the app (delete and download again). If the issue persists, please email us with your crash details.

**Q: Privacy & Data**  
A: We prioritize your privacy. Most of our apps function locally. For details, please view our [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).

## 📡 App Specific: EMF Detector Pro

**Q: Accuracy of readings?**  
A: The app uses the raw magnetometer data from your device. Metal cases or magnetic accessories may interfere with accuracy.

**Q: Is Ghost Mode real?**  
A: Ghost Mode is an entertainment feature designed to visualize sensor data in an immersive way.

**Q: How to save recipes?**
