## 2. Information We Collect

The type of information we collect depends on how you interact with our Services and which specific App you are using.

### A. Information You Provide to Us

- **Feedback & Support:** If you contact us via email or support forms, we may collect your name, email address, and the content of your message to verify your identity and respond to your request.
- **User Content:** Some Apps may allow you to create or upload content (e.g., notes, photos, recipes). This data is generally stored locally on your device or in your personal iCloud/Google Drive, and we do not have access to it unless you explicitly share it with us.

### B. Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Apps, certain information may be collected automatically by us or our third-party service providers, including:

- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (e.g., IDFA/AAID), and language settings.
- **Usage Data:** Information about how you use the App, such as features used, time spent, and crash logs. This helps us fix bugs and improve user experience.
- **Location Data:** We do not collect precise GPS location. However, general location (e.g., city/country level) may be inferred from IP addresses for analytics or ad localization purposes.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- To provide and maintain our Services.
- To improve our Apps and develop new features.
- To fix technical issues and monitor App stability (e.g., Crashlytics).
- To display non-personalized or personalized advertisements (if the App is ad-supported).

We may employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our Services ("**Service Providers**"). These third parties may have access to your personal information only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

Key third-party services we may use across our Apps include:

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these third-party service providers.

## 5. Data Retention and Security

- **Retention:** We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy.

Our Services are generally intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

- **For "Kids" Category Apps:** If we publish an App specifically targeted at children, we will ensure it complies with COPPA and GDPR-K, including limiting data collection and disabling behavioral advertising.
